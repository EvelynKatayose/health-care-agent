Privacy notice

Updated October 19, 2026. Health Care Agent is a personal health project with owner-only access. This English notice describes the same data practices as the Chinese interface.

Data and purposes

After you authorize access, this project reads available Oura sleep, readiness, activity, workouts, stress, blood oxygen, heart health, sessions and tags. You can sync 7, 30, 90, 180 or 365 days; heart rate samples are limited to the last 7 days. Availability depends on membership, permissions, device and uploads from the Oura app.

Oura records, developer credentials, access tokens, journal entries (including period dates, flow and symptoms you enter), chat text and personal context are encrypted on the server and isolated by signed-in account. The encryption key is stored separately in runtime configuration. Older browser-local entries upload only if you choose to import them.

AI processing

When you send a question, the app sends your question, current attached photo, recent messages in the selected conversation, health summaries for the selected range, recent journal entries, personal context and relevant cycle records to the OpenAI API. Report-related conversations can include a computed structured report. Oura credentials and tokens are not sent to the model. Requests use store=false; this does not promise that the provider retains no technical or security logs.

Meal analysis sends the photo and/or food description you submit to OpenAI to estimate calories and nutrients. Photos are used for the current request and are not stored in this project's database. Chat history retains text and an attachment flag, not the photo itself; later conversations cannot view earlier photos. Confirmed meal estimates are saved only when you choose to save them. The app does not fabricate replies when the model is unavailable.

Control, export and deletion

You can export health summaries, recent journal entries, cycle records and reports. You can delete individual journal or cycle entries and conversations, or edit and clear personal context. Choosing “Save to context” adds selected conversation text to your personal context. Deleting the Oura connection removes this project's developer app details, tokens and stored Oura data; it does not remove journal entries or chats. Revoke this app's permission in your Oura account to revoke access there as well.

Records remain until you delete them. Hosting and model providers handle backups and technical logs under their own policies. Switching languages does not translate or replace saved personal records. This project does not automatically access the original ChatGPT account's conversation history or Memory.
